How to choose

Le Ticket leans hardest into the loyalty story (it literally looks like a punch-card + arcade ticket) — warm, human, unmistakably a café. Bold Play is the most "wow, not usual" and the easiest to make feel premium + playful at once. Arcade is pure fun and game-forward, but it's dark — best as the *games zone* inside a lighter app. L'Atelier is the most sophisticated/artistic — big serif + riso texture, feels like a design studio made it.

My pick for you: Bold Play as the backbone (confident, unusual, works light) with Le Ticket's stamp/punch-card motif for the loyalty card itself, and Arcade reserved for the game/spin moments. That combo is special AND coherent. — Tell me which one speaks to you, or say "mix 1 + 2" and I'll develop the full system.
