# Admin (shop owner) · mobile

**"Admin" here means the shop owner** — the person who runs the café and stands
at the counter. Not the platform operator; that console is a different tool.

**Who I am.** I run a small café. I have one phone and no computer at the shop.
My phone is the till, the calculator, the music and the camera. During service my
hands are wet, I'm holding a cup, and there is someone waiting.

**How this sheet was made.** The real app driven on a 390×844 phone viewport,
screen by screen, including a real credit of 60 dinars to a real customer.
Everything described was on the screen. Nothing was fixed along the way.

*Fixture noise to ignore later: the shop is "Café Test", its type shows as
"Autre" because the fixture never sets one, and the account is on a trial.*

---

## 1 · Where I come in

**How I heard about it.** A friend sent me a link, or I typed the domain.

**What I see.** The sales page. **"Une seule carte. Toutes vos récompenses."**,
*"Scannez. Cumulez des points. Revenez quand vous voulez. Sans application."*, a
violet **"Commencer gratuitement"**, and under it *"14 jours gratuits • Sans
carte bancaire"*. Then a strip: **UTILISÉ PAR · Cafés · Restaurants · Barbiers ·
Boutiques · Salons**, then "Comment ça marche". The page is long — about 4700px
of scroll.

**How I feel.** "Sans carte bancaire" is the line that keeps me reading. Every
other thing I've been sold wanted a card before it wanted to be useful.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *14 days, then what? I have to scroll a long way to find the price.*
- *Is this Tunisian? "Support en Tunisie" is somewhere down the page but not up here.*

---

## 2 · Créer mon compte

**What I see.** **"Créez votre compte"**, *"Quelques secondes, et vos clients
peuvent scanner."*, a violet note *"✦ 14 jours gratuits — sans carte bancaire."*
Then **E-mail** (placeholder `vous@boutique.tn`), **Mot de passe** with a
**VOIR** toggle, *"8 caractères minimum."*, **"Créer mon compte"**, and *"Déjà un
compte ? Se connecter"*.

**How I feel.** Short. Two fields. Nothing asked for my business name, my address
or my trade — that all comes later, which is the right order.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *"vous@boutique.tn" — I don't have a business email. Is Gmail fine?*
- *What happens right after? Do I get an SMS, a mail, nothing?*

---

## 3 · Se connecter

**What I see.** **"Bon retour 👋"**, *"Connectez-vous pour gérer vos points,
votre caisse et vos récompenses."*, E-mail, Mot de passe with **VOIR**, **"Se
connecter"**, and *"Pas encore de compte ? Créer un compte"*.

**How I feel.** Fine. It's a login.

---

## 4 · The till — the screen I will actually live in

**What I see.** A header: a **C** avatar, **Café Test**, *"Espace café"*, and on
the right a small badge — **"Essai · i▮"** — because the round avatar circle sits
on top of it and cuts the word "illimité" in half. Then:

- two tabs: **Un client** (selected) / **Un code**
- a wide violet **"Scanner le QR"** button
- a field: *"Code client ou numéro"*
- a **numeric keypad**: 1–9, 0, ⌫
- a **"Chercher"** button
- **"Mes clients · 1"** and a chip: **Y · Yassine · à l'instant**
- a bottom bar: **Caisse · Analyses · QR · Réglages**

**How I feel.** This is right. Big targets, no typing on a phone keyboard while
someone waits, and the whole thing reachable with one thumb. It looks like a
till, not like a website.

**What I do.** The customer says four characters. I tap them. Tap **Chercher**.

**The one thing I keep noticing.** The trial badge is broken. Every screen in the
owner app carries that header, so I see a chopped-off word all day.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *"Un client" / "Un code" — which one do I want when someone shows me a reward?*
- *"Scanner le QR" opens the camera — will it ask permission every time?*
- *"Mes clients" shows one person and "à l'instant". Is that a list of everyone, or only recent?*

---

## 5 · The customer appears — the counter screen

**What I see.** The whole screen is taken over. Nothing else is on it:

- **Yassine** in large type
- under the name, the customer's four-character code (mine showed as **V▮▮UA** — see below)
- **Solde 10 points**
- a huge amount display showing **0**
- *"Montant en dinars · 1 pt/DT"*
- a big keypad: 1–9, `.`, 0, ⌫
- a wide **Créditer** button, greyed until I type something
- a quiet link: **Corriger / Historique**

**How I feel.** This is the best screen in the product. It is one job with one
button. The rate is printed right there — "1 pt/DT" — so I never have to
remember it, and I never type points, only dinars. That means I cannot cheat and
I cannot fumble a conversion in front of a queue.

**The defect.** The customer's code under their name is set in Space Mono at
weight 800. Space Mono has no 800, so the browser fakes it, and at that size the
wide letters — M, W — fill in and become solid blocks. I read a code as
"V ▮ ▮ U A". If I ever needed to read that code back to the customer, I couldn't.

**What I do.** Type **60**. The label under the display changes live to **"→ 60
points"**. Tap **Créditer**.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *"Corriger / Historique" is the smallest text on the screen and it's the undo. Should it be that quiet?*
- *If I typed 600 instead of 60, would anything stop me?*

---

## 6 · After crediting

**What I see.** A green bar appears under the balance: **"+60 points · nouveau
solde 70 points"**. The **Solde** line at the top updates to **70**. The amount
display resets to **0**. The keypad and **Créditer** are still there, ready.

**How I feel.** Exactly what I need. It tells me what happened *and* where the
customer now stands, and it's already ready for the next person. I never had to
dismiss anything.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *That green bar — how long does it stay? If the next customer walks up, am I still looking at the last one's balance?*
- *Does the customer see this on their phone straight away?*

---

## 7 · The "Un code" tab

**What I see.** **"Valider un code"**, *"Le code que le client montre sur son
téléphone."*, a field, and a **"Vérifier"** button.

**How I feel.** Clear once I'm here. But I only found it because I tapped the
second tab out of curiosity. Nothing on the "Un client" side told me that
redeeming a reward happens elsewhere.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *What does a valid code look like? Four characters like the client code, or something else?*
- *After I verify it — do I hand over the coffee and it's done, or do I have to confirm again?*

---

## 8 · Analyses

**What I see.** **"Analyses"**, *"Est-ce que vos clients reviennent ?"*, three
range tabs — **7 jours / 30 jours / Tout** (30 selected) — and then a single
card: a sparkle, **"Pas encore de client"**, and *"Posez votre QR sur le
comptoir. Dès la première carte, vous verrez ici s'ils reviennent."*

**How I feel.** Confused for a moment, because the Caisse screen right next door
says **"Mes clients · 1"** and shows Yassine by name. One screen says I have a
customer; this one says I have none.

I can guess the rule — this page probably counts customers who have *spent*, not
customers who have *joined* — but nothing on the page says so, and the two
screens use the same word for two different things.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *Do I have a customer or not?*
- *"Est-ce que vos clients reviennent ?" is the right question. When do I get an answer — after 2 people? 5?*

---

## 9 · Mon QR

**What I see.** **"Mon QR"**, *"Posez-le sur vos tables. C'est par là que tout
commence."* Four format tabs, each with a subtitle:

- **Chevalet** *Sur la table* (selected)
- **Affiche A5** *Vitrine, mur*
- **Autocollant** *Comptoir*
- **Story** *Instagram*

Then a preview of the actual thing: the shop's sparkle logo, **Café Test**,
**CARTE DE FIDÉLITÉ**, **"10 points offerts à l'inscription"**, a real QR,
**"Scannez pour commencer"**, *"Sans application · en 10 secondes"*, and the URL
`www.pointili.online/e2etest`. Under it: **Imprimer** · **Télécharger**, and
further down **Partager** · **Copier le lien**.

**How I feel.** This is the screen that turns a subscription into something
physical. Naming the formats by *where they go* rather than by paper size is the
right call — "Sur la table", "Comptoir" is how I think, not "A5".

**The practical problem.** **Imprimer** and **Télécharger** on a phone. I don't
have a printer connected to my phone, and I'm not sure what "Télécharger" leaves
me with or where it goes. This is the one task in the whole owner app that wants
a computer, and I'm being offered it on a phone.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *Where does the downloaded file go, and how do I get it to a print shop?*
- *Can I just send it on WhatsApp?* ("Partager" is there, but below the fold.)

## 10 · Réglages

**What I see.** The shop at the top: logo, **Café Test**, *"Autre ·
pointili.online/e2etest"*. Then grouped rows, each with a chevron:

**VOTRE PROGRAMME**
- **Les points** — *1 pt par dinar*
- **Les récompenses** — *4 visibles · dès 40 pts*
- **Carte à tampons** — *désactivée*

**VOTRE BOUTIQUE**
- **Nom, logo & type** — *Autre*
- **Adresse de la carte** — */e2etest*

**VOTRE COMPTE**
- **Formule** — *Essai* · a green **illimité** pill
- **Identifiant** — my email, in mono, running right to the edge of the card

**FORMULES** — two price cards: **6 mois · 65 TND** *(≈ 11 TND / mois)* and **1
an · 80 TND** *(≈ 7 TND / mois)*, then *"Tout est compris : points, récompenses,
tampons, analyses et le kit QR pour vos tables. Écrivez-nous pour renouveler — on
active votre compte le jour même."*

Then **Se déconnecter**.

**How I feel.** I like that every row shows its current value on the right — I
can read my whole configuration without opening anything. Showing the price in
TND per month next to the total is honest and it's the number I actually compare.

**What bothers me.** "Écrivez-nous pour renouveler" — *where?* There is no phone
number, no WhatsApp, no address on this screen. The one moment I'm ready to pay,
the app tells me to write to someone it doesn't name.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *My shop type says "Autre". I run a café. Where do I change that — "Nom, logo & type"?*
- *"Écrivez-nous" — to whom?*
- *If my trial ends while I'm not looking, do my customers' points disappear?*

---

## The whole thing, from my side

**What worked.**
- The counter screen. One customer, one number, one button, the rate printed on it, and a result that tells me the new balance. I could hand this to a new employee with no training.
- I type **dinars, never points**. That removes the two mistakes I'd actually make.
- The keypad. No phone keyboard during service.
- The QR kit named by where it goes, with a real preview.
- Réglages shows every setting's current value without opening it.

**What didn't.**
- The trial badge is cut in half by the avatar on **every screen** in the app.
- The customer's code is unreadable — Space Mono asked for a weight it doesn't ship.
- "Mes clients · 1" and "Pas encore de client" are on adjacent screens about the same person.
- "Écrivez-nous pour renouveler" names no contact anywhere.
- Print and download live on the phone, which is the one device that can't do either well.
- "Corriger / Historique" — the only undo — is the smallest text on the busiest screen.
- Redeeming a reward is behind a second tab that nothing points to.

**The question I never got answered.** *What happens the day my trial ends?* The
badge says "Essai · illimité" (when I can read it), the settings say "Écrivez-nous",
and nothing tells me whether the shop goes dark, whether my customers lose their
points, or how much notice I get.
