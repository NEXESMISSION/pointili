# Admin (shop owner) · desktop

**"Admin" here means the shop owner** — the person who runs the café. Not the
platform operator; that console is a different tool.

**Who I am.** Same owner. It's Sunday evening, the shop is shut, and I'm at home
on a 1440×900 laptop. This is the only time I do the *thinking* parts: set up the
account, decide the rewards, print the QR for the tables. During service I'm on
my phone; here I'm doing paperwork.

**How this sheet was made.** The real app driven on a 1440×900 viewport, screen
by screen, including a real credit of 60 dinars. Everything described was on the
screen. Nothing was fixed along the way.

*Fixture noise to ignore later: shop named "Café Test", type shows as "Autre",
account on a trial.*

---

## 1 · The sales page, on a real screen

**What I see.** The hero fills the fold: **"Une seule carte. Toutes vos
récompenses."**, the sub-line about no application, **"Commencer gratuitement"**,
*"14 jours gratuits • Sans carte bancaire"*, and a phone render on the right.
Below the fold: **UTILISÉ PAR · Cafés · Restaurants · Barbiers · Boutiques ·
Salons**, then "Comment ça marche", and a long way further, the price.

**How I feel.** It reads like a product, not a template. On the laptop I actually
read the whole page instead of thumbing past it — which means I get to the price
before I sign up, and 80 TND a year is not a decision I need to think about.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *Is there a demo I can look at before giving an email?*
- *Who runs this? There's a footer, but I want a name and a phone number before I put my shop on it.*

---

## 2 · Créer mon compte

**What I see.** The same phone-width card, centred, on the dark page: **"Créez
votre compte"**, *"Quelques secondes, et vos clients peuvent scanner."*, the
trial note, **E-mail** (`vous@boutique.tn`), **Mot de passe** with **VOIR**, *"8
caractères minimum."*, the button, and *"Déjà un compte ? Se connecter"*.

**How I feel.** Two fields on a 1440px screen is a lot of empty page, but I don't
mind — I'd rather a short form than a long one. Typing an email with a real
keyboard is the reason I'm doing this at home instead of at the counter.

---

## 3 · The till, on a laptop

**What I see.** This is where desktop earns its keep. A proper **left sidebar** —
**Caisse** (highlighted) · **Analyses** · **QR** · **Réglages** — with the shop's
**C** avatar and "Café Test / Espace café" at the top, and at the bottom-left a
small **"Essai · illimité"** badge, which here is fully readable (on the phone it
is cut in half by the avatar).

The content sits in a column of roughly 680px starting after the sidebar:

- tabs **Un client** / **Un code**
- **Scanner le QR**
- the *"Code client ou numéro"* field
- the numeric keypad, now with buttons about 210px wide
- **Chercher**
- **Mes clients · 1**, the *Y · Yassine · à l'instant* chip, and a **"Rechercher…"** box

Right of that column: about **260px of empty background**, all the way down.

**How I feel.** The navigation is a genuine improvement — I can see all four
sections at once instead of a bottom bar. But the keypad is strange here. I have
a keyboard in front of me. Ten large buttons for digits I could type is the phone
layout scaled up, not a desktop layout.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *Can I just type the number and press Enter? Nothing tells me.*
- *Why is a third of the screen empty when there's a customer list that could sit beside the keypad?*

---

## 4 · A customer at the counter — the takeover

**What I see.** The whole till dims and a **480px column** appears in the middle
of the 1440px screen:

- **Yassine**
- their four-character code underneath
- **Solde 70 points**
- a large **0** display
- *"Montant en dinars · 1 pt/DT"*
- the keypad again — 1–9, `.`, 0, ⌫
- **Créditer**
- **Corriger / Historique**

Everything left of x≈480 and right of x≈960 is a dimmed, unusable copy of the
till. Below the panel, about 300px of empty dark.

**How I feel.** The *logic* is exactly right — one customer, one amount, one
button, the rate printed on it, and no way to type points. I would defend that
design anywhere.

But on this screen it is unmistakably a phone panel dropped onto a monitor. The
dimming makes it worse, because it turns two-thirds of my display into greyed-out
noise I'm being told not to look at.

**What I do.** Type 60. The label updates to **"→ 60 points"**. Click
**Créditer**.

**What I see next.** A green bar: **"+60 points · nouveau solde 70 points"**. The
balance line updates. The amount resets to 0. Still ready for the next person.

**How I feel about that.** Perfect feedback. It says what happened and where the
customer stands, in one line, without a dialog to dismiss.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *Why is the undo — "Corriger / Historique" — the smallest text in the panel?*
- *On a desktop, could I not see the customer's history right there instead of behind a link?*

## 5 · Analyses

**What I see.** Sidebar, then **"Analyses"**, *"Est-ce que vos clients reviennent
?"*, three range tabs (**7 jours / 30 jours / Tout**, 30 selected), and one card:
a sparkle, **"Pas encore de client"**, *"Posez votre QR sur le comptoir. Dès la
première carte, vous verrez ici s'ils reviennent."*

Below that card: roughly **530px of empty page**.

**How I feel.** Two things at once. First, the same contradiction as on the
phone — the Caisse page says **"Mes clients · 1"** and names Yassine, this page
says I have none. I can guess the rule (this probably counts customers who have
*spent*, not who have *joined*) but the page never says it, and both screens use
the word "client".

Second: this is the page I came to the laptop *for*. Analysis is a desktop task.
And it's one small card in the top-left eighth of my screen.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *Do I have a customer or not?*
- *When this fills up, will it use the space? Or is this going to be one narrow column of numbers on a wide screen?*

---

## 6 · Mon QR — and the one that's actually broken

**What I see.** **"Mon QR"**, *"Posez-le sur vos tables. C'est par là que tout
commence."*, and the four format tabs — **Chevalet** *Sur la table* (selected),
**Affiche A5** *Vitrine, mur*, **Autocollant** *Comptoir*, **Story**
*Instagram*.

Then the preview, and **the preview is cut off at both ends.**

The card begins mid-sentence: the top line reads **"offerts à l'inscription"** —
the shop's logo, the shop's name and the words **CARTE DE FIDÉLITÉ** and the
start of "10 points" are all sliced off above the card's edge. At the bottom, the
line **"Scannez pour…"** is chopped through the middle of the letters. The QR
itself is intact in between.

Underneath: **Imprimer** · **Télécharger** · **Partager** · **Copier le lien**,
then a link **"Voir ce que voit un client →"**, then a section **"Où le
mettre"**.

**How I feel.** This stops me. I am about to print the thing that goes on every
table in my shop, and the preview of it is missing its top and its bottom. I
don't know whether the *printed* version is fine and only the preview is broken,
or whether I'm about to print a chopped card and take it to a print shop and pay
for it.

That uncertainty is expensive in a way nothing else in the app is: everything
else I can undo.

**What I do.** Click **"Voir ce que voit un client →"** because I no longer trust
the preview, and I want to check the QR actually opens my shop.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *Is the print broken or is the preview broken?*
- *Should I download it and open the file before I spend money at the print shop?*
- *Does each of the four formats have the same problem?*

**Note for whoever reads this later:** on the phone the same preview renders
complete — logo, name, "CARTE DE FIDÉLITÉ", the headline, the QR, "Scannez pour
commencer", "Sans application · en 10 secondes" and the URL. The clipping is a
desktop-only thing.

## 7 · Réglages

**What I see.** Sidebar, then the shop header — logo, **Café Test**, *"Autre ·
pointili.online/e2etest"* — and grouped rows, each showing its current value on
the right:

**VOTRE PROGRAMME**
- **Les points** — *1 pt par dinar* ›
- **Les récompenses** — *4 visibles · dès 40 pts* ›
- **Carte à tampons** — *désactivée* ›

**VOTRE BOUTIQUE**
- **Nom, logo & type** — *Autre* ›
- **Adresse de la carte** — */e2etest*

**VOTRE COMPTE**
- **Formule** — *Essai* + a green **illimité** pill
- **Identifiant** — my email in mono

**FORMULES** — **6 mois · 65 TND** *(≈ 11 TND / mois)* and **1 an · 80 TND** *(≈
7 TND / mois)*, then *"Tout est compris : points, récompenses, tampons, analyses
et le kit QR pour vos tables. Écrivez-nous pour renouveler — on active votre
compte le jour même."*

Then a full-width **Se déconnecter**.

**How I feel.** The best-organised screen in the app. Every setting shows its
value without me opening it, so I can audit my whole configuration in one look.
The price cards give me the per-month figure next to the total, which is the
number I actually compare against other things I pay for.

**What stops me again.** *"Écrivez-nous pour renouveler"* — there is no phone
number, no WhatsApp, no email, nowhere on this page. This is the exact screen and
the exact moment where I have decided to pay, and the product doesn't tell me
whom to pay or how to reach them.

**What I'm asking myself.**
- *Écrivez-nous — where?*
- *My type says "Autre". I run a café. Is that under "Nom, logo & type", and why wasn't I asked at signup?*
- *"on active votre compte le jour même" — so this is manual, a person does it. Which person?*

## The whole thing, from my side

**What worked.**
- The sidebar. Four sections visible at once beats a bottom bar for the setup work I came here to do.
- The counter logic survives the change of device intact: dinars in, points calculated, rate printed, result stated with the new balance.
- The trial badge is fully readable here, unlike on the phone.
- Réglages shows every value without opening anything, and prices are shown per month as well as per term.
- Naming the QR formats by *where they go* — table, wall, counter, Instagram.

**What didn't.**
- **The QR preview is clipped top and bottom on desktop.** It is the one thing I'm about to spend money printing, and I can't see it whole. The phone shows it complete.
- The customer panel is a 480px phone column floating in a dimmed 1440px screen.
- "Mes clients · 1" and "Pas encore de client" are one click apart, about the same person, using the same word.
- Analyses — the reason to open a laptop at all — is one small card with 530px of empty page under it.
- A ten-button touch keypad on a machine with a keyboard, and no hint that I can just type.
- "Écrivez-nous pour renouveler" names no contact anywhere in the product.
- Roughly a third of the window is empty on every owner screen.

**The question I never got answered.** *Am I about to print a broken card?* I
came to the laptop to do one physical, irreversible, money-spending task, and
that is the one screen that gave me a reason to doubt it.
